# A finite seed can have a nonseparable spectrum

2026-11-18. Status: PROOF_RECONSTRUCTED; independent audit requested. This gives a concrete obstruction to treating the separable-spectrum repair as automatic, and another intrinsic subobject outside M. Its sigma-Boolean pushout target is also outside M, by the subsequently established strong factorization theorem. No equalizer counterexample for M is obtained.

Let J be a discrete set of cardinality c, and let K=J union {infinity} be its one-point compactification. Put

    S=Cont(K,Omega_discrete)

with M_1 acting by postcomposition. Every element is constant outside a finite subset of J. Hence |S|=c, S is finite-support, and S has no M_1-fixed point. Its equivariant character space H(S) is canonically K, by the ordinary finite-partition/Stone evaluation argument. In particular H(S) is compact and nonseparable: every isolated point of J must belong to a dense subset.

## 1. One observation function can separate all M-orbit types

Choose countably many binary functions f_n:S->2 which jointly separate S, possible because |S|<=c. Choose injections b_n:Omega->Omega with pairwise disjoint images. The subsets b_n S are pairwise disjoint: their elements are functions on the nonempty space K whose ranges lie in the corresponding disjoint subsets of Omega. Define

    h(b_n s)=f_n(s),

and give h an arbitrary value on the remaining points of S. Then h·b_n=f_n for every n. Thus the full M-orbit of the single function h:S->Omega separates all points of S.

For the diagonal C_bfs-object Y=(S --id,id--> S), the sort-0 type quotient generated by the single seed h is therefore S itself. Its character space is the nonseparable K. Consequently the separability hypothesis in [the finite-support completion theorem](finite_support_type_quotients_are_m_epic_completions_2026-11-18.md), Section 6, does not follow merely from a finite seed, finite support, or the bound |Q_0|<=c.

## 2. The sigma-algebra of character observations is proper

Let Sigma be the sigma-algebra on S generated by all evaluation functions

    ev_x:S->Omega,       x in K.

Every Sigma-measurable Omega-valued function depends on only countably many evaluation coordinates. This follows from closure of the countably supported sets under complements and countable unions, and then from the countably many fibers of an Omega-valued function.

No countable family of evaluation coordinates separates S: choose an isolated point of J outside that family and alter a continuous function only at that point. In particular h from Section 1 is not Sigma-measurable. Otherwise h would depend on a countable set L of evaluation coordinates, and every h·m would depend on that same L, contradicting separation by the orbit of h.

Define a subpresheaf A of E(Y) by

    A_0=Omega^S,
    A_1={Sigma-measurable functions S->Omega},
    p_A=q_A:A_1 -> A_0 the inclusion.

It has finite tuple coding, left joint separation, first-sort FS-separation, and truncation closure. Its balanced elements in either sort are precisely the evaluation functions ev_x, x in K, all of which belong to A_1. Thus A is E(sigma)-orthogonal; the existence condition is nonvacuous and includes the entire nonseparable character space K.

## 3. The sort-0 seed has no monic D-cover in A

We first use a direct ZFC lemma. If a monomorphism of M_2-sets

    j:Omega^Q -> Omega^S

has image containing a countable point-separating family on S, then j is onto. Its Boolean restriction is a sigma-complete Boolean embedding: coordinate M_2-maps correspond to countably complete ultrafilters, as in the scalar argument of [the finite-image note](finite_image_intrinsic_subobject_counterexample_2026-11-18.md), Section 3. Thus the image contains every singleton {s}, obtained by a countable intersection of fibers of the separating functions. The image Boolean algebra is isomorphic to P(Q), hence atomic. Each of its atoms must be a singleton, because all singletons already belong to the image. Conversely every singleton comes from an atom. The resulting bijection Q<->S shows that the image contains every subset of S, and then every Omega-valued function, by its countable fiber partition. This also identifies j as an E-image of the corresponding bijection.

Suppose now j:E(Q)->A is monic and its sort-0 image contains h. It contains the countable separating family h·b_n, so the lemma makes j_0 an isomorphism onto Omega^S. Right equivariance identifies Q_0 with S as an M_1-set.

Monicity of p_A and the equation j_0 E(p_Q)=p_A j_1 imply that p_Q is onto. Since Q belongs to C, every character of Q_0=S descends along p_Q. The evaluations ev_x jointly separate S, so p_Q is also injective. Hence E(p_Q) and j_0 are isomorphisms, forcing the image of p_A j_1 to be all of Omega^S. This contradicts A_1!=Omega^S.

Therefore A is outside M in ZFC. This argument does not need N or the bounded compression theorem.

This illustrates why adding all the required E(sigma)-extensions is not the same as placing them in one full-power monic cover. The first sort already contains every function on S; the second sort contains every character and its countable measurable combinations, but still omits the orbit-separating seed h.

## 4. The pushout target is also excluded

Form the sigma-Boolean pushout

    B=P(S) coproduct_Sigma P(S)

with embeddings alpha,beta and the codiagonal r:B->P(S). Give it the induced right M_1 action. The P-object

    T_0=Omega^S,       T_1=Gamma(B),       p_T=q_T=Gamma(r)

receives two maps E(Y)->T which are the identity in sort 0 and alpha,beta in sort 1. Strong amalgamation makes their equalizer precisely A.

The earlier countably generated Borel ghost-atom obstruction does not directly apply: Sigma has no countable point-separating family, and finite points of S cannot be isolated by countably many character observations.

Nevertheless the [strong packed-separator factorization theorem](packed_separators_force_monic_d_factorization_2026-11-18.md) excludes T from M. Both sorts of Y have size c, are fixed-point-free, and have packed countable separators by Section 1. If T belonged to M, the displayed equalizer would belong to D. It is A, which is outside M by Section 3, a contradiction. Thus T is outside M in ZFC.

Its exact failure of the weaker intrinsic conditions has not been identified here. Existence of balanced extensions follows from the common evaluations, but uniqueness could still fail through additional balanced elements in Gamma(B). Nor has H_fg membership been decided. These questions are separate from the already established M-nonmembership.
