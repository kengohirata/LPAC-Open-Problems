# A Borel subobject satisfying truncation closure but outside M

2026-11-18. Status: PROOF_RECONSTRUCTED; independent audit requested. This strengthens the finite-image subobject obstruction: truncation closure and nonvacuous E(sigma)-orthogonality still do not force membership in M. The direct proof below assumes N; the established both-finite-support compression removes N for the M-nonmembership conclusion.

Let I=2^omega, with its standard Borel sigma-algebra Sigma, and put

    Y=(I times Omega --id,id--> I times Omega),

where M_1 acts on the Omega coordinate and fixes I. Then Y belongs to C, because Y_0 is finite-support and p_Y is the identity. Thus E(Y) belongs to D.

Let A be the subpresheaf with both sorts the Borel functions

    A_i={f:I times Omega->Omega : f is Borel},

and p_A=q_A=id. Postcomposition and the right precomposition actions preserve Borel functions.

## 1. All the proposed intrinsic and truncation conditions hold

Finite tuples and all finite pointwise operations preserve Borel measurability. Left joint separation and first-sort FS-separation are inherited from E(Y). If every e_n f is Borel, each positive fiber of f is Borel, and its zero fiber is the complement of their countable union. Thus A is truncation-closed.

E(sigma)-orthogonality is nonvacuous here. A balanced element f must satisfy f(i,m(n))=m(f(i,n)); constant m force f(i,n)=n. Conversely the function (i,n)->n is Borel and balanced. Each sort therefore has exactly one balanced element, and p_A=id identifies them.

Fix a bijection c:Omega^2->Omega and define the Borel seed

    h(i,n)=c(n,i(n)).

Its row functions h_i:Omega->Omega are nonconstant, and distinct i give distinct row functions. The ambient subpresheaf generated by h is finitely generated.

## 2. Under N, no full-power map into A can contain h

Suppose theta:E(Q)->A has h in its sort-1 image, with Q arbitrary. Compose it with A->E(Y). Under N, full faithfulness gives an actual K-map

    q:Y->Q,       theta=E(q) with codomain restricted to A.

Let k:Q_1->Omega satisfy k q_1=h. For each i, restrict q_1 to the M_1-orbit {i} times Omega. A quotient of the simple M_1-set Omega is either Omega or a singleton. Since h_i is nonconstant, this restriction must be injective, with image an Omega-orbit O_i in Q_1.

If O_i and O_j intersect, equivariance under the constant maps shows that q_1(i,n)=q_1(j,n) for every n. Applying k gives h_i=h_j, so i=j. Therefore the O_i are pairwise disjoint.

Choose a non-Borel subset S of I. For U=union_(i in S) O_i subset Q_1,

    chi_U q_1 = chi_(S times Omega).

The left side belongs to the image of E(Q)->A and must be Borel. Restricting to the Borel section i->(i,0) would make S Borel, a contradiction.

Thus, under N, no map from any full power E(Q) into A can contain h. In particular A is outside H_fg, hence outside M. The proof does not require theta to be monic.

## 3. Removing N for M-nonmembership

Assume only ZFC, and suppose a monomorphism E(Q)->A contains h, with Q in C. Since p_A=q_A=id, monicity implies p_Q=q_Q is onto. Consequently Q_1 is finite-support, as a quotient of Q_0. Passing to the diagonal object (Q_1 --id,id--> Q_1) gives a monic D-subobject with the same sort-1 image, still containing h.

Apply the established [both-finite-support epi-compression theorem](both_finite_support_accessibility_and_epi_compression_2026-11-18.md) to the fg seed. It yields a further monic D-witness with both dual carriers at most c. One may again pass to its diagonal quotient as above.

For an index set of cardinality at most c, every M_2-map Omega^S->Omega is coordinate evaluation in ZFC. Indeed such maps correspond to countably complete ultrafilters; a nonprincipal one would give an uncountable measurable cardinal at most |S|, whereas every measurable cardinal is strong limit and strictly exceeds c. Thus the composite of this compressed witness with E(Y) is E(q) for an actual K-map q:Y->Q, without N.

The argument of Section 2 now applies unchanged, giving the same non-Borel-union contradiction. Hence A is outside M in ZFC, conditional only on the already established compression theorem, not on N or NC. The stronger assertion A is outside H_fg was proved above under N; no ZFC proof of that stronger assertion is claimed here.

## 4. Interpretation

This is an intrinsic, truncation-closed subobject of a D-object, with a balanced element in each sort, which is not locally covered by monic D-images. Thus neither truncation closure nor nonvacuity of E(sigma)-existence repairs the stronger relative-subobject conjecture.

The sigma-algebra is closed under countable unions but cannot contain all unions of the distinct row-type blocks forced by h. This realizes exactly the uncountable complete-atomic-subalgebra obstruction identified in [the sigma-Boolean reduction](truncation_closed_subobjects_and_sigma_boolean_reduction_2026-11-18.md).

This does not exhibit A as an equalizer with codomain in M. A concrete sigma-Boolean amalgam realizes it as an equalizer, but that target is itself outside M; see [the ghost-atom obstruction](borel_amalgam_target_ghost_atom_obstruction_2026-11-18.md).
