# Small character spaces and separated second sorts force D-factorization

2026-11-18. Status: PROOF_RECONSTRUCTED. The coordinating agent proposed the ordinal application, and mono_equalizer independently reconstructed the factorization argument and this generalization. No external review or formal verification. All statements are ZFC.

Use the standing C, D, M and E, and c=2^aleph_0. A set with M_1-action is **Ω-cogenerated** here if its equivariant maps to Ω separate points, equivalently if its evaluation map into Ω^(H_1(S)) is injective.

## 1. The source theorem

Let Y∈C satisfy

    |Y_0|≤c,
    0<|H_1(Y_0)|≤c,
    Y_1 is Ω-cogenerated.                              (1)

Then every map a:E(Y)→Z with Z∈M factors through a monic D-subobject of Z. More precisely, there is one first-sort function z_0:Y_0→Ω such that **any** monic D-cover of a_0(z_0) contains the image of the whole map a.

Consequently every pair E(Y)⇉Z with Z∈M has its equalizer in D.

The proof uses [continuum Boolean continuity](continuum_boolean_continuity_and_orbit_separator_factorization_2026-11-18.md), Theorem 1, and the small-source covered-separator factorization argument. The feature beyond finite orbit-separation is that C's unique-character-extension condition places an entire character family in one cover automatically.

## 2. The first-sort cover contains all character images

Since H_1(Y_0) is nonempty, Y_0 has no M_1-fixed point. The size bound lets us choose a countable point-separating family on Y_0 and pack it into one z_0. For any monic cover

    j:E(T)↪Z,       T∈C,

of a_0(z_0), the one-sort version of the covered-separator argument gives an equivariant map τ_0:T_0→Y_0 with

    a_0(f)=j_0(fτ_0)       for every f:Y_0→Ω.             (2)

This one-sort argument needs no bound or separator on Y_1.

For h∈H_1(Y_0), let h̃∈H_1(Y_1) be its unique extension along p_Y. The function hτ_0 is equivariant on T_0. Since T∈C, it has a unique equivariant extension h'_1:T_1→Ω along p_T.

Both j_1(h'_1) and a_1(h̃) are balanced elements of Z_1 with p_Z-image a_0(h). E(σ)-orthogonality of Z gives

    a_1(h̃)=j_1(h'_1).                                  (3)

Thus every second-sort character image lies in this same cover j, even though they need not be generated by z_0 under the ambient unary operations. There are at most c such characters.

## 3. The second dual map exists

For t∈T_1 put w=j_1(χ_t)≠0. For each h̃∈H_1(Y_1), let p_(h̃)=h'_1(t). By (3),

    w≤a_1[h̃=p_(h̃)]       for every h̃.

Y_1 has no fixed point because its character space is nonempty. Continuum Boolean continuity applies to a_1. Hence

    w≤a_1χ_F,
    F={y∈Y_1:h̃(y)=p_(h̃) for every h̃∈H_1(Y_1)}.       (4)

The family has size at most c. Since Y_1 is Ω-cogenerated, F is empty or a singleton. It cannot be empty because w≠0. Define τ_1(t) to be its unique point. Then

    h'_1=h̃τ_1       for every second-sort character h̃.  (5)

Each h'_1 is equivariant. Applying the separating characters to τ_1(mt) and mτ_1(t) proves that τ_1 is equivariant.

Naturality for p follows from h'_1p_T=hτ_0 and character separation. For q, use (2), (3), naturality of a and j, and monicity of j_0:

    j_0(h'_1q_T)=q_Zj_1(h'_1)
                 =q_Za_1(h̃)
                 =a_0(h̃q_Y)
                 =j_0((h̃q_Y)τ_0).

Thus h̃τ_1q_T=h̃q_Yτ_0 for every h̃, proving τ_1q_T=q_Yτ_0. We have constructed an actual K-map τ:T→Y.

## 4. Whole-map equality: a second cover excludes ghosts

Let a'=jE(τ). We know a'_0=a_0 and a'_1(h̃)=a_1(h̃) for all second-sort characters. This does not by itself justify equality on arbitrary functions by checking only the atoms of j.

Suppose a_1(f)≠a'_1(f). Choose another monic D-cover k:E(U)↪Z containing the three elements

    a_0(z_0),       a_1(f),       a'_1(f).

The first-sort argument again puts the whole common map a_0=a'_0 inside k_0. The unique-extension argument again puts all common character images a_1(h̃)=a'_1(h̃) inside k_1.

Choose a point of U_1 where the two lifts of f differ and let w be its nonzero image atom. Read the common character profile at that point. Apply c-meet preservation to a_1 and a'_1 separately. As in (4), both resulting profile fibers are the same singleton {y}, and

    w≤a_1χ_y,       w≤a'_1χ_y.

On that singleton f has the same value f(y) for both maps. Finite conditional identities therefore contradict the choice of the differing lifts. Hence a=a', proving the factorization theorem.

The new cover in this step is essential: it contains the putative difference, while C automatically brings in every common character image. This avoids assuming that the atoms of the first cover are globally join-dense in Z.

## 5. Equalizers and a spectral formulation

For two maps a,b, take one cover j containing both a_0(z_0) and b_0(z_0). The preceding proof produces explicit K-maps τ_a,τ_b:T⇉Y with a=jE(τ_a), b=jE(τ_b). Form their K-coequalizer Y→Q. Since T,Y∈C and C is colimit-closed, Q∈C. Thus

    Eq(a,b)≅E(Q)∈D.

No full-faithfulness assumption is used.

There is a useful version of (1) requiring only character data. If both Y_0 and Y_1 are Ω-cogenerated and

    0<|H_1(Y_0)|≤c,

then |Y_0|≤c follows automatically. Indeed, finite support makes X=H_1(Y_0) a compact Stone space, and every evaluation y↦(h↦h(y)) is continuous X→Ω. A compact Hausdorff space with at most c points has a base of size at most c; its clopen subsets, being compact, are finite unions of basic opens. It therefore has at most c continuous Ω-valued functions. Injectivity of evaluation gives the desired bound on Y_0.

For completeness, the base bound can be proved by choosing separating neighborhoods for each ordered pair of distinct points, then taking finite intersections: compactness of the complement of an open neighborhood makes these a base. This uses at most c choices.

## 6. Ordinal sources of cardinality at most c

Let |κ|≤c and consider

    Y_κ=(C([0,κ],Ω) --inclusion,d_κ^*--> Ω^[0,κ]).

Its first carrier has at most c elements: the ordinal compactum has a base of size at most c, hence at most c clopens and continuous Ω-valued maps. Its first character space is [0,κ], and evaluations separate both sorts.

The fact that Y_κ∈C is ZFC at this size. Every countably complete ultrafilter on a set of size at most c is principal: embed the set in 2^ω and intersect the countably many chosen bit fibers. Thus the full-power character classification on the second sort has only evaluations. The usual compact-space character calculation gives the same evaluations on the first sort.

All hypotheses of the theorem hold. Hence every E(Y_κ)→M factors through a monic D-subobject, and every pair with this source and an M-codomain has its equalizer in D. The stronger initial-ordinal-seed rigidity conclusion uses the separate propagation argument through limits of cofinality at most c.

This covers uncountable ordinals of cardinality at most c. It does not apply merely from a bound on |Y_1|, nor does it settle general C-surjectivity repair or unrestricted M-equalizer closure.
