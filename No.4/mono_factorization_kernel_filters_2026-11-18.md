# Kernels of D-maps into H_fg: countably complete filters

2026-11-18. Status: PROOF_RECONSTRUCTED. This is a structural lemma, not a proof of M=H_fg, filtered-colimit closure, or a Problem 4 counterexample. No independent review or formal verification.

Use Ω=ω, P, C, D=E(C^op), H_fg, and the mono-factorization candidate M from [the candidate note](mono_factorization_candidate_2026-11-18.md). In particular, E(Y)_i=Ω^{Y_i}.

The finite tuple-coding and jointly point-separating unary-family lemmas in [the equalizer analysis](general_equalizers_finite_orthogonality_and_quotient_reduction_2026-11-17.md), §§4 and 10, give a useful description of kernels. This does not solve the mono-factorization problem.

**Kernel-filter lemma.** Let u:E(Y)→Z with Z∈H_fg. For each sort i there is a countably complete filter F_i on Y_i, possibly the improper filter, such that for f,g:Y_i→Ω,

    u_i(f)=u_i(g)  iff  {y∈Y_i : f(y)=g(y)}∈F_i.

Thus the componentwise image is the reduced power Ω^{Y_i}/F_i as a set with all its finitary Ω operations. This statement concerns the kernel; it does not assert that these images belong to D or M.

### Proof

Use two distinct elements 0,1∈Ω and write χ_A for the characteristic function of A⊆Y_i. Define

    F_i={A⊆Y_i : u_i(χ_A)=u_i(1)}.

Finite tuple coding shows that u_i preserves every finitary pointwise operation on Ω: code its finitely many inputs by a fixed bijection Ω^n≅Ω, then apply the corresponding unary selfmap. Consequently its kernel is a congruence for all such operations.

The whole set belongs to F_i. Closure under intersections follows by applying Boolean conjunction to χ_A and χ_B. If A⊆B and A∈F_i, apply Boolean disjunction with χ_B to obtain χ_B congruent to 1. Thus F_i is a filter, allowing the improper case.

If f and g are congruent, apply the binary equality test to (f,g) and (f,f); this gives χ_{f=g} congruent to 1. Conversely, if χ_{f=g} is congruent to 1, apply the operation t(b,x,y)=x for b=1 and t(b,x,y)=y otherwise. Pointwise t(χ_{f=g},f,g)=g, whereas t(1,f,g)=f. Hence f and g are congruent.

To prove countable completeness, suppose A_n∈F_i for n∈ω, and put A=⋂_n A_n. Define h:Y_i→ω by h(y)=0 on A, and h(y)=1+min{n:y∉A_n} elsewhere. For each k∈ω let m_k:ω→ω fix 0,...,k and send all larger integers to 0. The family (m_k)_k is jointly point-separating, and

    {y:m_k(h(y))=0}=⋂_{n<k} A_n∈F_i.

Therefore m_k(u_i(h))=m_k(u_i(0)) for every k. The jointly point-separating-family lemma for H_fg implies u_i(h)=u_i(0). By the established equality-set characterization, A={h=0} belongs to F_i. □

The right M_1 actions and the two diagram arrows impose additional inverse-image compatibility conditions on these filters, directly from naturality of u. No classification of the compatible filters is claimed here. Countable completeness by itself supplies neither a uniform cardinal bound on C-witnesses nor a D-subobject covering a prescribed finite family in the image.

## A small image obstruction inside the actual D

The compatible filters can be principal even when their image fails H_fg. This already occurs for a map between two actual D-objects, without N or the No.1 cardinality input.

Let

    B=(Ω⇉1),    T=(1⇉1),    A=B⊔T,
    Z_*=(Ω --id,id--> Ω).

The M_1 actions on the singleton sets are trivial. Both T and Z_* lie in C. The object A also lies in C: A_0=Ω⊔1 is finite-support, and H_1(A_0)=H_1(A_1)=∅ because each contains an M_1-fixed point, whereas Ω has no M_1-fixed point.

Let s:Z_*→B have components id_Ω and !, and let b:B↪A be the coproduct inclusion. The C-to-C map

    h=b s:Z_*→A

has ambient image B∉C. Dually,

    E(A) --E(b)--> X=E(B) --E(s)--> E(Z_*)

is the regular-epi/mono image factorization of E(h). Indeed, E(b) is componentwise restriction of functions to a subset and is surjective, while E(s) is componentwise injective. Its kernel filters are the principal filters generated by the B_i summands inside A_i.

Nevertheless X∉H_fg. One direct ZFC check uses σ:U→B from the earlier note, with U=(Ω² --π_0,π_1--> Ω). All H_fg objects are orthogonal to E(σ):E(B)→E(U), since both endpoints are ambient finitely generated. For X, the required extension of id_X would correspond to a K-map B→U, but its sort-1 component would be an M_1-map 1→Ω, which does not exist. The required correspondence is ZFC here because the coordinate sets B_i,U_i are countable.

Consequently:

- The actual D is not closed under images of its own morphisms.
- Even a map D→Z with Z∈D⊆M may have image outside H_fg.
- The kernel-filter lemma cannot be upgraded to image membership, even for principal filters.

This does not give a separation M≠H_fg: in this example the image X is covered by the surrounding D-object E(Z_*). It identifies exactly why proving image membership is stronger than, and cannot replace, the desired local monic-cover statement.
