## Problem by problem

*Note: Bibliographic references below are still being compiled and may be incomplete.*

**No.1.** *(Summary)* Is every small-orthogonality class in the dual of an LP category reflective? Equivalently, is the class of objects coorthogonal to a *set* of morphisms always coreflective?
- **Literature:** "Yes under VP" is remarked in the book (p. 295).
- **AI notes (unverified):** Claim independence from ZFC, with a lower bound of a proper class of measurable cardinals (giving a negative answer in $V=L$ and $L[U]$) and upper bounds of WVP, a proper class of almost strongly compact cardinals, or a proper class of $\Pi_2$-strong cardinals. Exact strength remains open in the notes.

**No.2.** *(Summary)* Is each orthogonality class in the dual of an LP category (a) reflective, or (b) an intersection of two full reflective subcategories? (Test morphisms may form a proper class.) Subcategories are read as replete (closed under isomorphisms). Without this, (b) holds trivially; see [No.2/README.md](No.2/README.md).
- **Literature:** "Yes under VP" is known.
- **AI notes (unverified):** Address (b), and claim independence from ZFC with a lower bound of a proper class of measurable cardinals (claiming a counterexample when no uncountable measurable cardinal exists) and an upper bound of WVP (obtained by claiming (a) under WVP).

**No.3.** *(Summary)* Can the definition of a locally generated category be stated without co-wellpoweredness? More precisely, is every cocomplete category with a strong generator formed by generated objects LP?
- **Literature:** Börger–Tholen (1990), Ex. 4.3, is a related construction; a full literature check is pending.
- **AI notes (unverified):** Claim that the answer is no in ZFC, by a counterexample: a category of ordinal-ranked bi-pointed sets. It is cocomplete and has a generated strong generator, but its initial object has a proper class of strong quotients.
  - The counterexample contradicts a density step on p. 296 of the book. The notes therefore labelled it only a candidate.
  - An AI cross-check (2026-11-20) confirmed the counterexample and located the error in that step of the book.
  - If this is right, the book's remark that the answer is yes under VP (p. 295) also fails for the literal statement.
  - The reformulation on p. 296 in terms of reflective subcategories is not refuted; under VP it is true.
  - The notes also give positive answers in some definable special cases.

  None of this has been checked by a human.

**No.4.** *(Summary)* Is every full subcategory of an LP category closed under limits and directed unions reflective?
- **Literature:** "Yes under WVP" is in the book.
- **AI notes (unverified):**
  - A positive answer if there are arbitrarily large measurable cardinals.
  - A definable counterexample in $V=L$ without inaccessible cardinals.
  - A counterexample when "small" is interpreted relative to the first inaccessible universe.

  Together, the first two give independence from ZFC relative to large cardinals. The exact strength remains open. The individual notes do not state the independence explicitly; it is summarized in [No.4/README.md](No.4/README.md).

**No.5.** *(Summary)* In $\mathbf{Ab}$: (a) is every full subcategory closed under limits reflective? (b) Is every full subcategory closed under products and split subobjects weakly reflective?
- **Literature:** Known; both (a) and (b) are equivalent to WVP. Part (a) is Przeździecki's solution to Isbell's problem. Part (b) follows by combining published results (the book's Thm 6.26 and Rem. 6.27, Wilson, and Casacuberta–Gutiérrez–Rosický) with an elementary step.
- **AI notes (unverified):** Assemble the published results and write out the elementary step for (b).

**No.6.** *(Summary)* In a locally $\lambda$-presentable category, is every full subcategory closed under $\lambda$-directed colimits cone-reflective?
- **Literature:** Known to be equivalent to VP. "Yes under VP" is the book's Cor. 6.7; a counterexample under $\neg\text{VP}$ is given by the discrete subcategory in the remark following Cor. 6.7.
- **AI notes (unverified):** Only check the closure condition for the remark's example.

**No.7.** *(Summary)* Is every full subcategory of an LP category closed under products and split subobjects an injectivity class?
- **Literature:** "Yes under WVP" follows from the book (Thm 6.26, Rem. 6.27) together with Wilson's results.
- **AI notes (unverified):** Claim independence from ZFC (exact strength open), proposing a lower bound of arbitrarily large measurable cardinals and an upper bound of arbitrarily large almost strongly compact cardinals. The lower bound comes from a counterexample built on $\operatorname{End}(\Omega)$-sets. The $\mathbf{Ab}$ example of No.5(b) only gives a counterexample when there is no uncountable measurable cardinal.

**No.8.** *(Summary)* In $\mathbf{Top}$: (a) is every full subcategory closed under products and directed colimits an injectivity class? (b) Is every full subcategory closed under limits an orthogonality class?
- **Literature:** (a) is known to be false (connected spaces give a counterexample; Adámek–Herrlich–Strecker, *Abstract and Concrete Categories*, Rem. 9.26). A literature check for (b) is pending.
- **AI notes (unverified):** Claim (b) is false in ZFC using a construction based on Koubek's stiff spaces, and claim that the restriction of (b) to compact Hausdorff spaces is equivalent to VP.

**No.9.** Duplicate of No.5, as noted in the authors' [errata](https://math.fel.cvut.cz/en/people/adamekj/corrections.pdf) (last line). See [No.5](No.5/).

**No.10.** *(Summary)* In an LP category, is every injectivity class closed under equalizers an orthogonality class?
- **Literature:** "Yes under WVP" follows from the book's Thm 6.22.
- **AI notes (unverified):** Claim equivalence to WVP, attempting the converse via Przeździecki's non-reflective orthogonality class in $\mathbf{Ab}$.

**No.11.** *(Summary)* (a) Is each accessible category co-wellpowered with respect to strong epimorphisms? (b) What is the precise status of the statement "every accessible category is co-wellpowered"?
- **Literature:** (a) is known to be true in ZFC (following from the book's Prop. 2.29 and the remark after Thm 2.33). For (b), known bounds are a proper class of measurable cardinals from below (the book, Ex. A.19) and a proper class of strongly compact cardinals from above (Makkai–Paré; Bagaria–Casacuberta–Mathias–Rosický).
- **AI notes (unverified):** Leave (b) open, but claim an improved upper bound of arbitrarily large almost strongly compact cardinals.

**No.12.** *(Summary)* In a $\lambda$-accessible category, is every $\lambda$-pure morphism a regular monomorphism?
- **Literature:** Known to be false in ZFC (Adámek–Hu–Tholen, 1996).
- **AI notes (unverified):** Record the known negative answer.

**No.13.** *(Summary)* Is every complete, axiomatizable category LP? (Here axiomatizable means equivalent to the category of models of a theory in some $L_{\lambda\lambda}$, with all homomorphisms.)
- **Literature:** The book notes that the answer is yes under VP, and yes under arbitrarily large compact cardinals (Rosický, 1994).
- **AI notes (unverified):** Attempt a proof in ZFC; the AI notes themselves rate the draft only as "probably correct". An AI cross-check (2026-11-20) found no fatal error. It noted that a key lemma in §4 was only sketched; that lemma is now written out in [No.13/section4_reduced_products_and_ranked_types.md](No.13/section4_reduced_products_and_ranked_types.md). The draft has not been checked by a human.

**No.14.** *(Summary)* Find a large accessible category $T$ that has a faithful accessible functor into an arbitrary accessible category.
- **Literature:** Literature check pending.
- **AI notes (unverified):** Interpret the target categories as large accessible categories and ask whether such a $T$ exists. Based on two unverified proof drafts, the notes claim independence from ZFC—specifically, no in $L$, in $L[A]$ for any set $A$, and in certain forcing extensions, and yes from one extendible cardinal.

**No.15.** *(Summary)* Is every finitely accessible category sketchable by a finite sketch? (Following later papers, "finite sketch" is read as a finitary sketch, where every specified diagram is finite; if the entire sketch must be finite data, the answer is no in ZFC by counting.)
- **Literature:** Known to be equivalent to "there is no uncountable measurable cardinal" (Adámek–Rosický, 1995; Adámek–Johnstone–Makowsky–Rosický, 1997).
- **AI notes (unverified):** Include an unverified attempt at an alternative derivation of the negative direction.

**No.16.** *(Summary)* (1) Is VP equivalent to WVP? (2) Is the existence of arbitrarily large measurable cardinals equivalent to WVP?
- **Literature:** Known to be no to both questions. (1) is answered in T. M. Wilson, *Weak Vopěnka's Principle does not imply Vopěnka's Principle* (Adv. Math., 2020). (2) is answered in T. M. Wilson, *The large cardinal strength of Weak Vopěnka's Principle* (arXiv:1907.00284), which shows that WVP is equivalent to "$\mathrm{Ord}$ is Woodin".
- **AI notes:** None. [No.16](No.16/) only lists the published references.
